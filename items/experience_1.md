
# Experience 1: My Journey into UX Design

## What did I do?
I started studying User Experience Design at Wilfrid Laurier University. Through this program, I have been learning about user research, graphic design, and how to create designs that are useful and easy to understand.

## When did I do it?
I started in September 2025, and I am currently in my second year.

## What was good about it?
I enjoyed learning new design skills and working on creative projects. I also liked seeing how my ideas could turn into actual designs.

## What was my favourite memory?
One of my favourite moments was completing my graphic design projects and seeing how much my design skills had improved.

## What didn't I enjoy?
Sometimes it was challenging to manage multiple assignments and deadlines, especially when different projects were due around the same time.

## My Grade
A - This experience has helped me become more creative and confident, and it is preparing me for my future career in UX Design.


# Experience 2: Learning Graphic Design and Photoshop

## What did I do?
I started learning Adobe Photoshop as part of my graphic design course at Wilfrid Laurier University. Most of our assignments require us to use Photoshop, which is completely new to me. Since I found it difficult to understand all the tools and features, I also enrolled in online courses to improve my skills and practice outside of class.

## When did I do it?
I started learning Photoshop in September 2026. I am currently taking graphic design this semester and will continue learning it next semester.

## What was good about it?
I enjoyed exploring different design techniques and learning how to turn my ideas into creative posters. I am slowly becoming more comfortable with Photoshop, and every assignment helps me learn something new.

## What was my favourite memory?
One of my favourite experiences was working on the Dead Air Guitars poster. I experimented with typography, colours, and layering to create a bold design. It was exciting to see my final poster after spending time learning how to use different Photoshop tools.

## What didn't I enjoy?
The most challenging part was learning Photoshop from the beginning. There are so many tools and options that I sometimes feel confused or frustrated. Even simple tasks can take a lot of time, but I keep practising and watching tutorials to improve.

## My Grade
B - Even though Photoshop is challenging for me, I enjoy learning something new. I know these skills will help me become a better designer in the future.

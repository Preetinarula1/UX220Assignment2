
# Experience 5: Learning Swimming

## What did I do?
I started learning swimming because I believe it is an important life skill that everyone should know. It is useful for personal safety, staying active, and building confidence in the water. I wanted to challenge myself by learning something new outside my studies.

## When did I do it?
I started learning swimming in June 2026.

## What was good about it?
I liked learning a new skill that can be useful throughout my life. Swimming also helped me understand the importance of patience, regular practice, and taking small steps to improve.

## What was my favourite memory?
My favourite part was seeing myself make progress as I learned new swimming techniques. It felt rewarding to notice improvements through practice.

## What didn't I enjoy?
Learning swimming was challenging in the beginning because it required coordination, breathing techniques, and confidence in the water. Some techniques took longer to learn than I expected.

## My Grade
A - I would give this experience an A because swimming is a valuable life skill. It has helped me become more confident and taught me that learning something new takes time and practice.


# Experience 3: Learning Swimming

## What did I do?
I decided to learn swimming because I believe it is an important life skill. Swimming is not only good for physical fitness but also helps us stay safe around water. I have been learning different swimming techniques and practising to become more comfortable in the water.

## When did I do it?
I started learning swimming recently and have been practising to improve my skills.

## What was good about it?
The best part of learning swimming is becoming more confident in the water. It is also a good way to stay active and learn something useful that I can use throughout my life.

## What was my favourite memory?
My favourite moment was when I started feeling more comfortable in the water and realized that I was making progress. It motivated me to keep learning.

## What didn't I enjoy?
In the beginning, learning the techniques and coordinating my breathing and movements was challenging. Sometimes it was frustrating when I couldn't do something correctly, but I kept trying.

## My Grade
A - I would give this experience an A because swimming is a valuable life skill. It has taught me patience, confidence, and the importance of regular practice.

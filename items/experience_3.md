
# Experience 3: My Journey as a Henna Artist

## What did I do?
I learned the art of applying henna (mehndi) and eventually turned this skill into a small side business. I take bookings for Indian weddings, festivals, and other special occasions. Even though I already have experience, I continue practising new designs and techniques to improve my work and stay updated with the latest trends.

## When did I do it?
I started learning henna a 9 years ago and continue to work as a henna artist today.

## What was good about it?
I love that I can use my creativity to make people happy on their special occasions. I also enjoy meeting different clients and understanding what kind of designs they prefer. This experience has helped me improve my creativity, patience, communication, and time management.

## What was my favourite memory?
One of my favourite parts is seeing my clients happy after completing their henna designs, especially during weddings. It feels rewarding when someone appreciates my work or recommends me to others.

## What didn't I enjoy?
Sometimes it can be tiring to work for long hours, especially during wedding seasons when I have multiple bookings. Another challenge is keeping up with new trends and learning detailed designs, which requires regular practice.

## My Grade
A - I would give this experience an A because it has allowed me to turn my creative skill into a small business. It has also taught me how to work with clients, manage bookings, and continuously improve my designs.

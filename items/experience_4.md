
# Experience 4: Volunteering with the UXDSA Club

## What did I do?
I joined the UXDSA Club at Wilfrid Laurier University as a volunteer on the Visual Design Team. Being part of this club gives me an opportunity to work with other students who share an interest in UX and design. I am learning how to collaborate with a team, share creative ideas, and contribute to visual design activities outside my regular coursework.

## When did I do it?
I joined the club in September 2026 and am currently volunteering as a Visual Design Team Member.

## What was good about it?
I like being part of a creative community where I can meet new people and learn from other students. It also gives me an opportunity to improve my teamwork, communication, and design skills.

## What was my favourite memory?
One of my favourite moments was attending my first team meeting. I was excited to meet other members and learn more about the club and how I could contribute.

## What didn't I enjoy?
Sometimes I feel nervous about sharing my ideas or speaking in front of a group. However, I see this as an opportunity to improve my confidence and communication skills.

## My Grade
A - I would give this experience an A because it allows me to grow as a designer, meet people with similar interests, and gain experience working in a creative team.
